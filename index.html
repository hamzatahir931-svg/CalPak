One-time setup

Connect Google Sign-In 🔥

To enable Google login, you need a free Firebase project. This takes ~3 minutes.

  1. Go to console.firebase.google.com
  2. Create a new project (name it "CalPak" or anything)
  3. Go to Authentication → Sign-in method → Google → Enable
  4. Go to Project Settings → Your apps → Add Web App
  5. Copy the firebaseConfig values below
  6. Also add localhost and your domain to Authorized Domains

Your config is saved locally on your device only. Never shared.
